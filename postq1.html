So here are the steps for posting middle school grades.

  1. Log into SIS.
  2. Click Gradebook:
  3. Press Scores on the left:
  4. Pick a class to post your grade:
  5. Under Grade Columns, choose Post Columns - Term:
  6. Under Term, choose Q1:
  7. Press Update Columns:
  8. Popup shows, choose Q1:
  9. Choose Terms for All Student:
  10. Press Next:
  11. Press Average Q1:
  12. Choose Class Absence Totals:
  13. Press Next:
  14. Press Finish:
  15. Press Post Grades...
  16. Choose Q1 and Term grades for all students...
  17. You will see a red thumbtack for a success post.

Please make sure every student has a grade!

Do this for all other classes and you can keep reposting until the deadline.
