Welcome to 6th Grade Math!

Objectives

Today, we will get to know one another, learn how to be ready for math class, set up our learning tools, and show what we remember from Grade 5 math.

Agenda
  • Welcome & Name Tents: Fold an 8.5 × 11-inch sheet into thirds so it stands. Write your name large, dark, and easy to read. At the bottom, add one sport or activity you enjoy.
  • Meet Our Class: Learn names, share interests, and review how our math community will work together.
  • Be Ready Every Day: Bring a notebook, paper, and a pencil to every class.
  • Take-Home Math Book: Receive an available IM book to keep at home and use for review.
  • Grade 5 Math Diagnostic: Show what you already know so your teacher can plan the right support.
  • DeltaMath Setup: Register for our class account.
  • First Math Activity: Begin learning together during the second half of class.
Homework

Bring your notebook, paper, and a pencil to the next class. Keep your assigned math book at home for review.

Language Objectives
  • I can introduce myself by displaying my name clearly and sharing one sport or activity I enjoy.
  • I can describe what I need to bring to math class each day.
